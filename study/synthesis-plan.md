# Synthesis Plan

Do not begin redesigning after the first session unless a critical defect makes continued testing impossible.

## 1. Normalize notes

For each participant, create anonymized observations using participant IDs.

Separate:
- **Observation:** what happened.
- **Interpretation:** what it may mean.
- **Evidence:** task outcome, assistance, rating, comments.

## 2. Build observation statements

Good:

> P02 paused on the time field and asked whether an exact time was required before noticing the unknown-time option.

Avoid:

> P02 hated the time field.

## 3. Group patterns

Potential categories may emerge around:
- policy selection;
- terminology;
- uncertainty;
- evidence upload;
- navigation;
- review/edit;
- confirmation;
- trust;
- accessibility.

Do not force the data into these categories if different themes emerge.

## 4. Convert patterns into findings

Each finding should include:

- title;
- observed behavior;
- participants/tasks affected;
- impact;
- severity;
- supporting evidence;
- design implication;
- confidence/limitations.

## 5. Prioritize

Prioritize by:
1. severity;
2. frequency;
3. importance of affected task;
4. confidence in the evidence;
5. implementation effort only after user impact is understood.

## 6. Redesign

For every design change, record:
- finding addressed;
- old behavior;
- new behavior;
- why the change is expected to help;
- what still needs validation.

## 7. Report limitations

Include whatever actually applies:
- small convenience sample;
- participant relationship to researcher;
- prototype fidelity;
- device mix;
- lack of real claim stakes;
- researcher/moderator bias;
- any mid-study prototype change.
