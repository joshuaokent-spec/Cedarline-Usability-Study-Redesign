# Task Scenarios

Use one fictional scenario across the session so participants can build context.

## Scenario

You were driving your **2023 Honda CR-V** when another vehicle bumped your car in a parking lot yesterday.

No one appears injured. The car can still move, but you are not completely sure whether it should be driven until it is checked.

You remember the parking lot but not the exact time of the incident. You do not have photos available right now.

You want Cedarline to contact you by text if possible.

Do not enter real personal information.

## Task 1 — Start the correct claim

> Start reporting this loss for the Honda CR-V and choose the type of event that best matches what happened.

Observe policy selection, loss-category understanding, and initial orientation.

## Task 2 — Report what you know and handle uncertainty

> Continue the report using the information you have. You know where it happened generally, but you do not know the exact time.

Observe whether participants can proceed without inventing exact information.

## Task 3 — Describe safety/damage and defer evidence

> Continue the report. No one appears injured. The vehicle can move, but you are not completely sure it is safe to drive. You do not have photos with you right now.

Observe uncertainty handling and whether people understand that evidence can be added later.

## Task 4 — Choose contact preference and review

> Ask Cedarline to contact you by text if that option is available, then review the report before sending it.

Observe contact preference, review scanning, and submission expectations.

## Task 5 — Correct something before submission

Before submission:

> Imagine you notice that one detail on the review page is wrong. Show me how you would go back and correct it, then return to review and submit the report.

Observe edit discoverability, state preservation, orientation, and confirmation understanding.

## Optional Task 6 — Save and resume

Use only if time allows:

> You need to stop before submitting. Show me what you would do if you wanted to save your progress and come back.

Do not imply cross-device resume if the prototype does not support it.
