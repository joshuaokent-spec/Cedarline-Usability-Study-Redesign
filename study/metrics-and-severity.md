# Metrics & Severity Rubric

## Task outcome

### Success
Completed independently without researcher direction.

### Success with assistance
Completed, but required moderator guidance beyond a neutral think-aloud prompt.

### Failure
Could not complete the task, completed the wrong task, or would have submitted materially incorrect information without intervention.

## Assistance level

- **0 — none**
- **1 — neutral prompt**
- **2 — directional hint**
- **3 — explicit instruction**

## Post-task ease

After each task:

> 1 = very difficult  
> 7 = very easy

This is a self-reported ease rating, not an objective usability score.

## Critical error

Mark a critical error when the participant would likely:
- submit the wrong policy/loss;
- invent required information because they believe uncertainty is not allowed;
- fail to discover a necessary path to correct an error;
- misunderstand submission as coverage approval;
- become blocked from continuing.

## Finding severity

Assign severity after synthesis.

### Severity 0 — Observation
No clear usability problem or no action needed.

### Severity 1 — Minor
Creates friction but does not meaningfully threaten task completion.

### Severity 2 — Moderate
Causes hesitation, backtracking, misunderstanding, or occasional assistance.

### Severity 3 — Major
Frequently blocks an important task, causes incorrect input, or requires moderator intervention.

### Severity 4 — Critical
Creates a serious safety/trust risk, prevents the core task, or could cause materially harmful misunderstanding.

## Evidence rule

A finding should document:
- observed behavior;
- affected task;
- frequency/pattern;
- user impact;
- supporting comments where permission allows;
- severity rationale.
