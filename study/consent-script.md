# Participant Consent Script

Read this before the session.

---

Thank you for helping with a usability study of a fictional insurance claim-reporting prototype.

I am testing the **design**, not you. There are no right or wrong answers, and difficulty with the prototype is useful information.

Your participation is voluntary. You can skip any question, take a break, or stop the session at any time.

I will use a participant ID such as **P01** in my notes rather than your name. Please do not enter real insurance, financial, medical, or other sensitive personal information into the prototype.

I would like you to think aloud as you work by saying what you are looking for, what you expect to happen, and anything that feels confusing.

### Notes

Do you agree to participate and allow anonymized written observations to be used in this portfolio case study?

### Optional recording

Only ask this if recording will actually occur:

Do you separately agree to audio and/or screen recording for research review?

Recording consent is optional. A participant may join the study without agreeing to recording.

---

Record consent before continuing.
