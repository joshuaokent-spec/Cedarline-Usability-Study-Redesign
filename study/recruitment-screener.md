# Recruitment Screener

## Purpose

Recruit a small convenience sample for a formative usability study of an insurance claim-reporting prototype.

This is not intended to produce a statistically representative sample.

## Screener questions

1. Are you 18 years old or older?
   - Yes
   - No → do not recruit

2. Are you comfortable using websites on a phone or computer?
   - Yes
   - Somewhat
   - No

3. Have you ever filed an insurance claim yourself?
   - Yes
   - No
   - Prefer not to say

4. Which device would you be comfortable using for the session?
   - Desktop/laptop
   - Mobile phone
   - Either

5. Are you available for a 35–45 minute moderated session?
   - Yes
   - No

6. Are you comfortable thinking aloud while completing fictional tasks?
   - Yes
   - Somewhat
   - No

## Sampling notes

A useful small sample can include a mix of:
- people with and without prior claim experience;
- desktop and mobile users;
- different levels of comfort with web forms.

Do not describe convenience participants as representative insurance customers.
