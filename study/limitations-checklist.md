# Study Limitations Checklist

Complete this after recruitment and sessions.

Potential limitations to report honestly:

- [ ] Small sample size
- [ ] Convenience sampling
- [ ] Participants known personally to the researcher
- [ ] Uneven desktop/mobile mix
- [ ] Few or no participants with real insurance-claim experience
- [ ] Prototype rather than production system
- [ ] Fictional scenario lowers emotional/financial stakes
- [ ] Researcher also designed the product
- [ ] Sessions not recorded
- [ ] Prototype changed during the study
- [ ] Accessibility needs not represented in the sample

Add any additional limitations that actually apply.

Do not use the limitations section to dismiss findings; use it to bound the claims that can responsibly be made.
