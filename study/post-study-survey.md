# Post-Study Survey

Administer immediately after the task sequence.

Use a **1–7 scale**:

1 = strongly disagree  
7 = strongly agree

## Statements

1. I understood what I needed to do at each step.
2. I felt comfortable continuing when I did not know an exact detail.
3. It was clear what information I could add later.
4. I felt confident I could find and correct a mistake before submitting.
5. The amount of information on each screen felt manageable.
6. I understood what would happen after I submitted the report.
7. Overall, the claim-reporting process felt easy to use.

## Open-response questions

1. What was the most confusing part, if anything?
2. What part of the experience was most helpful or reassuring?
3. If you could change one thing, what would you change?
4. Was there anything you expected to see but could not find?

## Reporting

With a sample of five, report individual ratings and simple descriptive summaries carefully.

Do not imply statistical significance or population-level conclusions.
