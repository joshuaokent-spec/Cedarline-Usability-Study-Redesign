Study design

One lightweight study, three research methods

01

Interview

Short pre- and post-task questions capture expectations, prior experience, confidence, and reasoning.

02

Usability test

Participants complete realistic claim-reporting tasks while the moderator observes behavior and assistance needs.

03

Survey

Post-task ease ratings and a short post-study questionnaire provide structured self-reported feedback.

Research flow

Planned session sequence

  1. Consent & contextExplain purpose, voluntary participation, privacy, and think-aloud expectations.
  2. Pre-task interviewUnderstand prior claim experience and expectations without teaching the interface.
  3. Five core tasksStart a claim, handle uncertainty, defer evidence, review/edit, and submit.
  4. Post-task ratingsCapture perceived ease immediately after each task.
  5. Post-study surveyMeasure clarity, confidence, information load, and overall ease.
  6. Debrief interviewAsk what was confusing, reassuring, missing, or unexpectedly difficult.
